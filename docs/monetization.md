# Opus Davi — Revenue MVP

## Objective
Turn the existing Bitcoin platform into a paid infrastructure product without promising mining profits or custody of customer funds.

## Offer
- **Free:** explorer, public network statistics, limited alerts.
- **Pro — R$49/month:** private dashboard, worker monitoring, fee alerts, mining/pool monitoring, historical metrics.
- **Operator — R$199/month:** multiple workers/pools, advanced alerts, API access, longer retention.
- **Business — R$799/month:** team access, higher API limits, priority support, custom monitoring.

## Revenue model
Subscription revenue is the primary model. Optional usage-based API charges can be added after the first paying customers validate demand.

## Important product boundaries
- No guaranteed returns.
- No fake hashrate, balances, blocks, shares, or profitability.
- No customer private keys stored by the service.
- Mining results depend on hardware, electricity, difficulty, fees, pool rules and market conditions.
- Production wallet/recovery functionality must remain self-custody and require explicit user action.

## Launch target
First milestone: 10 active users and 1 paying customer. Second milestone: 10 paying customers. Scale only after retention and willingness to pay are demonstrated.

## Pricing principle
Do not subsidize infrastructure indefinitely. Free access should have clear limits; paid plans should directly fund hosting, monitoring, support and development.
